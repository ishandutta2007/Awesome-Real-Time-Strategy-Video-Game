# Awesome-Real-Time-Strategy-Video-Game

# Top Real-Time Strategy Video Game Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  
*Focused on Base Building, Resource Management & Open-Source RTS Engines*  
**Last updated: October 2026**

This repository tracks notable **commercial RTS games** and **open-source projects** that deliver real-time strategy gameplay — base building, resource gathering, unit production, and tactical combat against AI or human opponents.

**Examples** include Age of Empires, StarCraft II, Warcraft III: Reforged, Command & Conquer Remastered, Company of Heroes 3, Total War: Warhammer III, Age of Mythology, Homeworld 3, Stronghold: Definitive Edition, and Northgard (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: RTS is a domain where open-source has produced remarkable results. **0 A.D.** stands as the most ambitious open-source RTS with 20+ years of development. **OpenRA** faithfully reimplements Command & Conquer with modern improvements. **Widelands** delivers Settlers-like gameplay, and **OpenHV**, **Spring Engine**, and **MegaGlest** provide engine foundations. This section is heavily expanded.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Age of Empires](https://www.ageofempires.com/)**  
  **The definitive historical RTS franchise** with Age of Empires II: Definitive Edition and Age of Empires IV. **The most accessible RTS for newcomers** — deep strategy, historical campaigns, and active multiplayer.

- **[StarCraft II](https://starcraft2.com/)**  
  **The gold standard for competitive RTS** — asymmetric factions, precise balance, and the world's most developed esports scene. **Free-to-play** with paid campaigns.

- **[Warcraft III: Reforged](https://playwarcraft3.com/)**  
  Blizzard's remaster of the classic RTS with updated visuals and classic gameplay. **The foundation for the MOBA genre** — custom maps spawned Dota.

- **[Command & Conquer Remastered](https://www.ea.com/games/command-and-conquer)**  
  EA's faithful remaster of Tiberian Dawn and Red Alert with modernized UI, multiplayer, and mod support. **The classic C&C experience** — beloved for its campy FMV cutscenes.

- **[Company of Heroes 3](https://www.companyofheroes.com/)**  
  Relic's tactical RTS with destructible environments, cover mechanics, and dynamic campaigns. **The best squad-based tactical RTS** .

- **[Total War: Warhammer III](https://www.totalwar.com/)**  
  **The definitive Total War experience** — turn-based strategy with real-time tactical battles. Immense campaign map, diverse factions, and epic battles.

- **[Age of Mythology](https://www.ageofmythology.com/)**  
  Age of Empires spinoff with mythological units and god powers. **The most creative AoE variant** — Retold edition remastered in 2024.

- **[Homeworld 3](https://www.homeworld3.com/)**  
  Fully 3D space RTS with tactical combat and cinematic presentation. **The best space-based RTS** — continues the legendary Homeworld saga.

- **[Stronghold: Definitive Edition](https://www.stronghold-game.com/)**  
  Firefly's remaster of the classic castle-building RTS. **The definitive castle sim** — economic and military strategy.

- **[Northgard](https://www.northgard.net/)**  
  Viking-themed RTS with territory control and survival mechanics. **The best modern RTS for newcomers** — accessible yet deep.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[0 A.D.](https://github.com/0ad/0ad)**  
  **The most ambitious open-source RTS**, GPL-2.0 licensed with **3,000+ GitHub stars** . **Free, open-source, historical RTS** — build cities, manage economies, and command armies across ancient civilizations . **20+ years in development** — the longest-running open-source RTS project . **Single-player campaigns, multiplayer, and AI opponents** . **The flagship open-source RTS** — demonstrates what community development can achieve . **Best for RTS enthusiasts wanting a complete open-source experience** .

- **[OpenRA](https://github.com/OpenRA/OpenRA)**  
  **Open-source reimplementation of Command & Conquer: Red Alert, Tiberian Dawn, and Dune 2000**, GPL-3.0 licensed with **15,000+ GitHub stars** . **Modernized engine with widescreen support, multiplayer matchmaking, and mod support** . **The definitive way to play classic C&C** — improved UI, balance, and online play . **Extensively moddable** — community projects include Tiberian Sun, Red Alert 2, and Dune 2 . **Best for classic C&C fans wanting modern multiplayer** .

- **[Widelands](https://github.com/widelands/widelands)**  
  **Open-source Settlers-like RTS**, GPL-2.0 licensed . **Build settlements, manage economies, and conquer territory** . **Single-player campaigns and multiplayer** . **The best open-source Settlers alternative** — 20+ years of development . **Best for players wanting economic/territorial RTS gameplay** .

- **[OpenHV](https://github.com/OpenHV/OpenHV)**  
  **Open-source RTS based on the Command & Conquer engine (OpenRA)**, GPL-3.0 licensed . **Original sci-fi setting with new units, factions, and campaigns** . **The most complete standalone OpenRA mod** . **Best for players wanting a fresh C&C-like RTS** .

- **[Spring Engine](https://github.com/spring/spring)**  
  **Open-source RTS engine powering several games**, GPL-2.0 licensed . **Used by Balanced Annihilation, Zero-K, and Beyond All Reason** . **Full 3D terrain, physics, and Lua scripting** . **The most capable open-source RTS engine** . **Best for developers building RTS games** .

- **[Beyond All Reason](https://github.com/beyond-all-reason/Beyond-All-Reason)**  
  **Open-source RTS built on Spring Engine**, GPL-2.0 licensed . **Total Annihilation-inspired gameplay** — massive scale, hundreds of units, and tactical depth . **The most popular open-source RTS by player count** — active multiplayer community . **Best for players wanting a Total Annihilation successor** .

- **[Zero-K](https://github.com/ZeroK-RTS/Zero-K)**  
  **Open-source RTS built on Spring Engine**, GPL-2.0 licensed . **Fast-paced, competitive RTS** with unique units and physics-based combat . **Active multiplayer community and ranked play** . **Best for competitive RTS players** wanting an open-source alternative to StarCraft II.

- **[MegaGlest](https://github.com/MegaGlest/megaglest-source)**  
  **Open-source RTS fork of Glest**, GPL-3.0 licensed with **7 factions and 100+ units** . **Fantasy/medieval setting** with mod support . **Cross-platform** (Windows, macOS, Linux) . **The most complete open-source fantasy RTS** . **Best for players wanting a Warcraft-like open-source RTS** .

- **[Warzone 2100](https://github.com/Warzone2100/warzone2100)**  
  **Open-source RTS with unit design and campaign**, GPL-2.0 licensed with **3,000+ GitHub stars** . **Unique feature: design your own units** — chassis, weapons, propulsion . **Post-apocalyptic setting with 3 campaigns** . **Active development since 1999** . **Best for players wanting customizable units and tactical depth** .

- **[OpenTTD](https://github.com/OpenTTD/OpenTTD)**  
  **Open-source transport simulation RTS**, GPL-2.0 licensed with **7,000+ GitHub stars** . **Not combat-focused** — build transport networks, manage finances, and compete . **The definitive transport RTS** — active for 20+ years . **Best for players wanting economic RTS gameplay** .

- **[OpenRCT2](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2)**  
  **Open-source theme park management RTS**, GPL-3.0 licensed with **15,000+ GitHub stars** . **Build and manage amusement parks** — not combat, but real-time management . **Best for players wanting creative management RTS** .

- **[UFO: Alien Invasion](https://github.com/ufoai/ufoai)**  
  **Open-source tactical RTS inspired by X-COM**, GPL-2.0 licensed . **Turn-based tactical combat with real-time geoscape** . **Base building, research, and alien technology** . **Best for X-COM fans** wanting an open-source alternative .

- **[Bos Wars](https://github.com/boswars/boswars)**  
  **Open-source futuristic RTS**, GPL-2.0 licensed . **Fast-paced gameplay with resource management** . **The most lightweight open-source RTS** . **Best for low-resource systems** .

- **[Glest](https://github.com/Glest/glest)**  
  **The original Glest engine** — predecessor to MegaGlest and ZetaGlest . **Best for understanding RTS engine architecture** .

**Frameworks for building custom RTS solutions**: Choose based on whether you want to play or build. **0 A.D.** for a complete open-source RTS with historical setting . **OpenRA** for classic C&C gameplay with modern multiplayer . **Beyond All Reason** for Total Annihilation-style massive-scale RTS . **Zero-K** for competitive, physics-based RTS . **MegaGlest** for fantasy/medieval RTS . **Warzone 2100** for unit customization and campaigns . **Spring Engine** for building custom RTS games . **Widelands** for Settlers-like economic RTS . Note that true AAA RTS production with cinematic campaigns, competitive esports balance, and global multiplayer infrastructure remains primarily commercial territory; open-source projects provide strong engine, gameplay, and multiplayer foundations that require community building for complete RTS experiences.

## Disclaimer

- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.
- RTS games often support mods and custom maps. **Verify mod sources** — malicious mods can compromise systems. Use official repositories and community-vetted sources.
- **Multiplayer RTS requires server infrastructure** — open-source games like Beyond All Reason and Zero-K maintain community servers. Self-hosting requires bandwidth and administration.
- **Open-source RTS projects vary in polish** — 0 A.D. is complete but still evolving; Warzone 2100 is mature; Spring Engine games require more setup . Evaluate against your expectations.
- **Some open-source games require original game assets** — OpenRA requires files from classic C&C games. It is an engine reimplementation, not a full game distribution .
- The open-source ecosystem provides strong engine, gameplay, and multiplayer foundations, but **cinematic production values, competitive esports balance, and global matchmaking infrastructure** remain primarily commercial offerings.
